Samosa Recipe

Dish:Vegetable Samosa
Servings:6-8
Prep Time:30 minutes
Cook Time:20 mintues

Ingredients

For the Dough:
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 4 tbsp oil or ghee
- 1/2 tsp salt
- Water as needed

For the Filling:
- 2 boiled potatoes, mashed
- 1/2 cup green peas
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- Salt and chili powder to taste
- 2 tsp oil for cooking

Instructions

1.In a bowl, mix flour, salt, and oill. Add water graually and knead into a smooth dough. Set aside for 20 minutes.
2.Heat oil in a pan, add cumin seeds. Then add peas, potatoes, and spices. Cook for mintues. Allow to cool.
3.Divide the dough into small balls. Roll each ball into a cicrle and cut into halves.
4.Shape each half into a cone and fill with the potato-pea mixture. Seal the edges
5.Heat oil in a deep pand and fry samosas until golden brown. Drain excess oil on paper towels.
6.Serve hot with chutney or sauce.

Notes

Tip:For crispier samosas, let the dough rest for at least 30 mintues before rolling
Variation:You can use minced chicken or paneer instead of vegetables for a different flavor.
